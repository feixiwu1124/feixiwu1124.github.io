Independent economic research

Why Xiashi lantern making is fading

Xiashi lanterns are a national intangible cultural heritage: paper panels pierced by hand, thousands of needle holes at a time. While the department evaluated Haining Xiashi Lantern Co., I interviewed two master artisans and the company's founder about apprenticeship, machines, and whether the craft still pays a living.

Finding 1

A municipal talent-support plan meant to last three years stretched to five, and young apprentices stopped staying.

Finding 2

Limited tourism promotion meant few visitors could tell hand-pierced work from cheaper souvenirs.

Result

Haining's own lantern festivals began relying on cheaper manufacturers in Sichuan instead of local artisans.

Written up in a three-page field report.
